# Totem Lumen 0.1.0-alpha.69

Minecraft 26.3 · Fabric · Java 25

- In Minecraft RGB, fire and soul fire already present when a Nether chunk loads now light nearby terrain sooner. Floors across section boundaries refresh when the light changes, and flames retain their own emission in dim surroundings.
- Adult and baby striders now sample lighting across their bodies, so fire, soul fire, lava and magma blocks can illuminate their feet and bodies at different strengths.
- Nether portals now cast purple light at their original emission level.
