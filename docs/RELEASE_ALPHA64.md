# Totem Lumen 0.1.0-alpha.64

Minecraft 26.3 · Fabric · Java 25

- In Minecraft RGB, players and mobs can light their surroundings with luminous items in either hand. Nearby holders beyond the previous four-light limit now contribute. Client-side held lighting also works on vanilla servers, and Totem Lumen servers can disable it.
- When a hand clips into a wall, the light stays on the holder’s side. Moving a light updates its source cell on the next client tick while light spreads to more distant surfaces. Nearby stationary lights keep their illumination.
- Large mobs can show different light colors and brightness across their bodies. First-person vignette brightness now follows both held and placed lights.
- Bright artificial lights roll off smoothly, preserving more detail where multiple colors overlap. Minecraft Pure keeps held lighting disabled. Held lights remain purely visual.
